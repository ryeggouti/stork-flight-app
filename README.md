# Stork Flight

A free arcade game for Android and the browser: fly a white stork between minarets and storm clouds over a Moroccan city at sunset, and race AI storks trained with reinforcement learning.

**[▶ Play in the browser](https://ryeggouti.github.io/stork-flight-app/)**

- **Solo:** medals at 10, 20, 30 and 40 minarets unlock new storks.
- **Race the AI:** four opponents, from Beginner to Seasoned.
- **Watch the AI:** see a trained stork fly on its own.

Every drawing and sound is made with code. It works offline, and the Android package is built with [PWABuilder](https://www.pwabuilder.com/).
