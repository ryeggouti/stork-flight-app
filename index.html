1v1 online

Race a friend on another phone. You both fly the same course at the same time, and the last stork flying wins.

How to play

  • Space ↑ click or tap: the stork flaps once.
  • Tap the screen: the stork flaps once.
  • Let go and it glides down. Gravity never stops.
  • Fly through the gap between the minaret and the storm cloud. Touch either one, the ground or the top of the sky, and it's over.
  • M sound on or off · Esc back to the menu
  • The back button closes a page or leaves a run.
  • Race the AI: a see-through stork flies next to you. It's an AI I trained, and it sees only 5 numbers. Pick your opponent, from Beginner to Seasoned.
  • 1v1: race a friend on another phone. One of you makes a room and shares the 4-letter code, the other joins. Same course, same time: the last stork flying wins.

Your stork

Win medals to unlock new storks. A medal on any level counts. Skins only change how your stork looks: the hitbox and the physics stay exactly the same.

About this game

The storks you race are AIs trained with reinforcement learning. The exact same rules run in a Python environment, where each one learned to fly from rewards alone: +1 for each minaret, −1 for a crash. Nobody told it to flap.

Same world, two players

The physics here match the training environment step for step: 30 steps per second, the same gravity, the same gaps. So your score and the AI's score mean the same thing.

The AIs in this game are real snapshots from training, running right in your browser. Beginner is an AI early in its training, and Seasoned is the best snapshot after up to a million steps of practice. Every opponent is a real point in the learning process; none of them was made worse on purpose.

Made from scratch

Every drawing is made with code, the storks, minarets and medina wall. Every sound is made with code too, using the Web Audio API, with no recordings. White storks really do nest on minarets in Morocco, which is where the idea came from.

I wrote it with help from an AI coding assistant. The project idea, the experiments and the decisions are mine.
